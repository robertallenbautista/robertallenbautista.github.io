Privacy Policy and Disclaimer

Last updated November 23, 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing on purpose. robertallenbautista.com has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, no comment section and no forms. I don't ask for your name, email address or anything else when you read it.

Two outside services are involved in showing you the pages:

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address to run and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

If you email me

If you write to info@robertallenbautista.com, I use your name, address and message only to reply. I don't sell it, share it or add you to a mailing list. Ask and I'll delete the conversation.

Please don't send Social Security numbers, tax returns or other sensitive documents by email. I can't review individual tax situations through this site.

Links to other sites

Articles link to the IRS, state revenue departments, Amazon, LinkedIn, Substack and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I don't control what they collect. Links to my books on Amazon are plain links, not affiliate links, and I don't earn a commission when you click them.

This site isn't directed to children under 13.

Disclaimer

Everything on this site is general, educational information. It isn't tax, legal or financial advice for your situation, and reading it or contacting me doesn't create a preparer, accountant or advisory relationship.

Tax rules change and depend on your facts. Check current guidance at irs.gov and your state revenue department, or talk to a qualified professional, before you act on anything you read here.

I'm a CPA candidate, not a licensed CPA. The views on this site are my own and don't represent any employer or organization.

Changes and contact

If what this site collects ever changes, for example by adding analytics or a newsletter signup, I'll update this page first and change the date at the top. Questions go to info@robertallenbautista.com.
