The books of Robert Allen Bautista

Robert Allen Bautista is a Kansas City tax educator who writes plain-English tax guides. His books for freelancers, solo owners and small business owners include The Small Business Tax Code, Unlocked and The Small Business Tax Calendar. Both were independently published in 2026 and are sold on Amazon in Kindle and paperback editions.

The first book explains the rules. The second turns them into a schedule. Read together, they cover what you can deduct, how your entity choice changes your bill, and what to do in which month so tax season does not surprise you.

About the author

Robert Allen Bautista spent four years at PwC in real estate tax and IT audit, served as Chief Accountant at a CPA firm, and has worked with more than 350 individual and small business clients. He holds an M.S. and B.S. in Accounting from Purdue University Global, holds an active PTIN and is a CPA candidate. He works in English and Spanish.

Questions about Robert Allen Bautista’s books

Who wrote The Small Business Tax Code, Unlocked?

The Small Business Tax Code, Unlocked was written by Robert Allen Bautista, a Kansas City tax educator. It is available as a Kindle eBook (ASIN B0HRR9LMBB) and as a 78-page paperback (ISBN 9798143300767).

Who wrote The Small Business Tax Calendar?

The Small Business Tax Calendar was written by Robert Allen Bautista. It is available as a Kindle eBook (ASIN B0HHX4SYDD) and as a 45-page paperback (ISBN 9798143305809).

Where can I buy Robert Allen Bautista's books?

Both books are sold on Amazon, and all of Robert Allen Bautista’s books are listed on his Amazon author page. His Goodreads author page is also available.

Who are the books written for?

Both books are written for freelancers, solo owners and small business owners who want plain-English explanations of the tax rules that affect them.

Are the books a substitute for tax advice?

No. The books are general information. Your own situation depends on your facts, your state and the year, so confirm important decisions with a qualified preparer or the IRS.
