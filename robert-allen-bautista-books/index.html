The books of Robert Allen Bautista

Robert Allen Bautista is a Kansas City tax preparer and accountant who writes plain-English tax guides. He is the author of two books for freelancers, solo owners and small business owners: The Small Business Tax Code, Unlocked and The Small Business Tax Calendar. Both were independently published in 2026 and are sold on Amazon in Kindle and paperback editions.

The first book explains the rules. The second turns them into a schedule. Read together, they cover what you can deduct, how your entity choice changes your bill, and what to do in which month so tax season does not surprise you.

About the author

Robert Allen Bautista spent four years at PwC in real estate tax and IT audit, served as Chief Accountant at a CPA firm, and has worked with more than 350 individual and small business clients. He holds an M.S. and B.S. in Accounting from Purdue University Global, holds an active PTIN and is a CPA candidate. He works in English and Spanish.
