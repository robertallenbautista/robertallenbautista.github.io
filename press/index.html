Long bio

Robert Allen Bautista is a tax preparer and accountant based in Kansas City. He helps individuals, freelancers, gig workers and small business owners understand their taxes and file them correctly, and he writes plain-English guides that answer the questions he hears most often.

Robert began his career at PwC, where he spent four years working in real estate tax and IT audit. He later served as Chief Accountant at a CPA firm, where he reviewed the work of six or more tax preparers. Over the years he has worked with more than 350 individual and small business clients, across Forms 1040, 1065, 1120, 1120-S and 1041, multi-state returns, payroll, IRS notices and penalty abatement.

He holds a Master of Science in Accounting and a Bachelor of Science in Accounting (cum laude), both from Purdue University Global, holds an active PTIN and is a CPA candidate. He works in English and Spanish.

Robert is the author of The Small Business Tax Code, Unlocked and The Small Business Tax Calendar. His articles appear on his website, blog, LinkedIn, Medium and Substack.
