About

I'm a Kansas City tax preparer and accountant. I spent four years at PwC in IT audit and tax, served as Chief Accountant at a CPA firm, and have worked with more than 350 individual and small business clients. I hold an M.S. in Accounting and am a CPA candidate.

Most of the people I help are freelancers, gig workers and small business owners who never got a straight answer about their taxes. I write plain-English guides that answer those questions: how much to set aside, whether an LLC should be an S corp, what you can actually deduct, and what to do when an IRS letter arrives.
