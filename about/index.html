About Robert Allen Bautista

Kansas City tax preparer, accountant and author.

Biography

Robert Allen Bautista is a tax preparer and accountant based in Kansas City. He helps individuals, freelancers, gig workers and small business owners understand their taxes and file them correctly, and he writes plain-English guides that answer the questions he hears most often.

Robert began his career at PwC, where he spent four years working in real estate tax and IT audit. That background in both tax and audit informs how he prepares returns: carefully documented and built to hold up if the IRS asks questions. He later served as Chief Accountant at a CPA firm, where he reviewed the work of six or more tax preparers.

Over the years he has worked with more than 350 individual and small business clients. His work covers Forms 1040, 1065, 1120, 1120-S and 1041, multi-state returns, payroll, IRS notices and penalty abatement. Many of his clients are self-employed people who never received a straight answer about self-employment tax, estimated payments, entity choice or what they can actually deduct.

Robert holds a Master of Science in Accounting and a Bachelor of Science in Accounting (cum laude), both from Purdue University Global. He holds an active PTIN and is a CPA candidate. He works in English and Spanish, and publishes some of his guides in Spanish.

He is the author of The Small Business Tax Code, Unlocked, a book that explains the deductions, strategies and IRS rules solo owners and growing businesses most often miss. His articles appear on his blog, on LinkedIn, Medium and Substack, and on his website, and they cover topics such as the 15.3% self-employment tax, LLC versus S corporation decisions, quarterly estimated taxes, rideshare and delivery driver taxes, and the state-line rules for people who live in Kansas and work in Missouri, or the reverse.

The best way to reach Robert is a message on LinkedIn. The articles on this site are general information and are not tax advice for your specific situation.
