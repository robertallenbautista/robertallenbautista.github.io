Free Tax Help in Kansas City and Lawrence for the 2027 Filing Season: VITA, AARP Tax-Aide, Clinics and IRS Free File

Most free tax sites are closed until winter. Here is how to find the real ones when they open.

By Robert Allen Bautista · Kansas City tax educator · Published 2026-11-25

Key takeaways

  • Free volunteer tax sites (VITA and TCE) are seasonal. Local 2027 sites and hours are not posted yet, so look them up in late January or February.
  • IRS Free File guided software is for an adjusted gross income of $95,000 or less, according to the IRS page as of October 2026. Free File Fillable Forms has no income limit. Both reopen in January 2027.
  • The UMKC tax clinic still exists at a new web address. It helps with IRS disputes and does not prepare returns.
  • Start at IRS.gov/freefile. The IRS says you will not get Free File if you go straight to a software company's site.

Returns for 2026 are filed in early 2027, and most of the free options are closed for the winter. Volunteer sites, Free File and MilTax all reopen in January or February. This guide explains who each option is for, what the programs say about who qualifies, and how to find a real site in Kansas City or Lawrence once the 2027 lists go up. I checked each program on its own website in November 2026, and I say where a detail is not published yet.

Three kinds of free help

People often mix these up, so here is the short version.

  • Volunteer sites prepare your return for you. These are VITA and TCE.
  • Free software lets you prepare your own return. That means IRS Free File, and MilTax for the military community.
  • Tax clinics represent you in a dispute with the IRS. They are not return preparers.

VITA and TCE: volunteer return preparation

VITA stands for Volunteer Income Tax Assistance. TCE is Tax Counseling for the Elderly. The IRS says it sets no program-wide income limit and that individual VITA sites may set their own. VITA generally focuses on people who meet the Earned Income Tax Credit income guidelines. The IRS says many sites also help people who generally earn about $70,244 or less, people with disabilities and people with limited English. TCE serves people 60 and older and specializes in pension and retirement questions.

Services vary by site. The IRS notes that what a site can handle depends on which volunteers are available.

How to find a site

  • IRS VITA/TCE locator at freetaxassistance.for.irs.gov, or call 800-906-9887. The IRS says the locator is updated regularly from February through April.
  • AARP Foundation Tax-Aide locator at aarp.org/money/taxes/aarp_taxaide/locations.html, or call 888-227-7669 between January and April. The IRS says most TCE sites are run by AARP.
  • United Way 211. United Way of Greater Kansas City says you can call 2-1-1 to be connected with a nearby free tax site, and that appointments are scheduled as they become available.

Kansas City

The University of Missouri Extension says it sponsors tax programs in partnership with agencies in the KC Metro Tax Coalition. Its Kansas City page says information for 2027 filing services will be shared soon, and its statewide program page says Missouri information will be posted in January 2027. On the last posted version, the guide for a volunteer-prepared return was income under $69,000. Treat that as last season's number until the 2027 page appears.

Lawrence

KU Law students ran a VITA program in Lawrence for the 2026 season. That season's page said taxpayers had to make less than $69,000 and not itemize deductions, and service was first-come, first-served. The page covers spring 2026 only, so check the KU Law VITA page for 2027 dates before you go.

I am not listing site addresses or hours here. None are published for the 2027 season yet, and they change every year.

What to bring

The IRS checklist for free tax preparation, last updated October 24, 2026, includes:

  • Original photo ID for you and your spouse if filing jointly, such as a driver's license, state ID, military ID or passport.
  • Social Security cards for you, your spouse and your dependents. If you do not have an SSN, bring an ITIN notice.
  • Birth dates for everyone on the return.
  • Wage and earnings statements such as W-2, 1099-R and 1099-MISC, plus SSA-1099 for Social Security benefits and 1099 interest and dividend statements.
  • Last year's federal and state returns, if you have them.
  • Bank routing and account numbers for direct deposit, such as from a blank check.
  • Your daycare provider's name, tax ID number and the total you paid, and Form 1095-A if you had Marketplace coverage.

To e-file a joint return, both spouses must be present to sign the required forms.

IRS Free File

On the IRS page last updated October 21, 2026, Free File had closed for the season and the IRS said to check back in January 2027. The limits shown on that page may change for the new season:

  • Guided tax software: for adjusted gross income of $95,000 or less.
  • Free File Fillable Forms: for any income level. It uses online forms, offers limited calculations, does not file state returns and cannot prepare an amended return.

Whichever you use, start at IRS.gov/freefile.

MilTax for the military community

Military OneSource offers MilTax, free tax software and consultations for active-duty service members, eligible family members, survivors and recent veterans up to 365 days after separation or retirement. Its page says the software will be available in January 2027, and you can call Military OneSource at 800-342-9647 to reach a tax consultant. That page is labeled as a test site, so confirm the details when the software opens.

Tax clinics for IRS disputes

A Low Income Taxpayer Clinic, or LITC, represents people in audits, appeals and collection. The IRS says services are free or for a small fee, that income generally must be below a set threshold (250% of the federal poverty guidelines on the IRS clinic list), and that the disputed amount is typically under $50,000. Each clinic decides eligibility on its own.

The IRS Publication 4134 list (revised January 2026) shows two clinics in Kansas City, Missouri:

  • UMKC School of Law LITC: 816-235-6201. The clinic's own page says it is not a tax preparation service and asks you to call for a telephone intake. Its page moved to law.umkc.edu/academics/clinics/tax-clinic.php, so older links to the previous address return a 404 error.
  • Legal Aid of Western Missouri LITC: 816-474-6750 or 800-990-2907.

The same IRS list has no Kansas entry. If you live in Lawrence or elsewhere in Kansas, call a clinic and ask whether it can take your case, or use the clinic finder at taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov/litc.

Avoiding "free" lookalikes

  • Find help through the IRS locator or the IRS Free File page, not through an ad or a search result that happens to say "free."
  • For Free File, the IRS says you won't get it if you go directly to a partner's commercial website.
  • Ask what the total will be before you hand over documents. VITA and TCE preparation is free, so a surprise fee is a signal to stop.
  • If you do pay a preparer, check the PTIN. My guide on ghost tax preparers walks through the red flags.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use a VITA site?

The IRS sets no program-wide income limit, and individual VITA sites may set their own. VITA generally focuses on people who meet the Earned Income Tax Credit income guidelines, and many sites also help people who generally earn about $70,244 or less, people with disabilities and people with limited English.

When do the free tax sites open for 2027?

Local 2027 sites and hours are not published yet. The IRS says its VITA/TCE locator is updated regularly from February through April, AARP lists January through April for its locator, and both IRS Free File and MilTax say they will be available in January 2027.

Does the UMKC tax clinic prepare my tax return?

No. The clinic's own page says it is not a tax preparation service. It represents low-income taxpayers in federal tax disputes such as audits and collection, and you start by calling 816-235-6201 for a telephone intake.

Can I use IRS Free File if I earn more than $95,000?

On the IRS page as of October 2026, guided Free File software was for an adjusted gross income of $95,000 or less. Free File Fillable Forms is for any income level, but it offers limited calculations and no state filing. Check the IRS page again in January 2027 for the new season.
